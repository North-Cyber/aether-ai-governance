## One-sentence takeaway

State what the work supports and where it stops.

## Scenario and scope

- What system or scenario is being considered?
- What is intended and out of scope?
- Who could be affected?
- What inputs, outputs, data, tools, or boundaries are known?
- Which details are assumptions rather than observed facts?

## Question being evaluated

Write one focused question that a reader could verify against the evidence.

## Method

Record the exact steps, prompt, environment, provider/model label if available, date, number of runs, and any relevant configuration. Do not infer metadata that was not saved.

## Evidence and findings

For each finding, include:

- Evidence ID and exact observable behavior.
- Why it matters and who may be affected.
- Severity and likelihood, with the basis and confidence.
- Whether the finding is observed, inferred, hypothetical, or unknown.
- What the evidence does not establish.

## Safeguards and owners

Describe practical controls. Label owners as proposed roles unless a real owner is explicitly assigned. State what evidence would demonstrate that each control works.

## Recommendation

Choose a clear disposition (proceed, proceed with conditions, hold, or do not proceed in current form) and explain conditions, unresolved issues, and re-review triggers.

## Limitations and next steps

List missing transcripts, model/version details, test variations, documents, stakeholder input, or technical evidence. Make the next action specific and achievable.

## Portfolio language check

Before publishing, ensure the case does not imply a real audit, broad model capability, measured impact, formal certification, or completed work that the evidence does not support.
