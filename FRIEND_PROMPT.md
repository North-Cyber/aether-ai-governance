## Maintain this project, do not restart it

AETHER is an independent AI governance and product risk learning portfolio, not a company, real-product audit, certification, or clinical service. Preserve the calm ivory, evergreen, sage, and slate design. Keep it static, portable, understandable, accessible, and beginner-maintainable. The canonical homepage is `index.html`; there is no build step or `dist/` directory.

## Preserve evidence boundaries

- R-01 reports notes about two responses that avoided an exact clinic-hours guess. Full transcripts and complete model/version metadata are missing. Do not invent or reconstruct them.
- Portfolio P-01 is a fictional shared-phone reminder review with a conditional recommendation; lab P-03 is the related test scenario. Lab P-01 is a different privacy scenario about another person's appointment.
- E-01 is the fictional CareGuide tabletop review. All planned tests remain unrun; all release gates remain open; risk likelihoods remain unknown. The hold recommendation reflects missing evidence, not an observed product failure.
- The CareNav evaluation lab has six fictional scenarios, including P-03 for shared-phone reminders. It accepts manually captured responses and human ratings, and exports evidence. It does not call a model, automatically validate an answer, or establish CareGuide launch readiness.
